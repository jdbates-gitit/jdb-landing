October field update / Since the last look

A little more of my game is coming back.

My latest 20 complete main-18 rounds at Willow Fork average 4.80 under par, compared with 3.85 under in the previous 20. That's almost a stroke a round. My average UDisc rating moved from 175.9 to 181.3 across those same windows. It's an encouraging stretch, even though I'm still below my 2024 annual rating average.

How I counted these rounds

This October 24 export contains 526 entries under my two player names: Jason and Jason Bates. I excluded every other player's scores and UDisc's par-reference rows. Of my entries, 502 complete the recorded layout, 22 are partial, and 2 have no hole scores. All 526 remain in my log totals; only completed layouts enter the rating summaries. Ratings are available for 215 completed scorecards, so the 180 average is not an average of all 526 entries.

The Willow Fork hole chart, practice targets, nine-hole averages and recent comparisons use 376 completed standard 18-hole, par-55 scorecards. Custom and pro-tee layouts, incomplete cards and the empty entries are excluded. A zero in this export means an unplayed hole, not a zero-stroke score. For every scored card I checked the total against its hole scores and the recorded score versus par against the par of the holes actually played.

I grouped Willow Fork, Willow Fork Park and Willow Fork DGC in the course list, and grouped the two Agnes Moffitt names. Other course names remain as exported; they may not all represent different physical courses. Monthly and yearly ratings span different courses and layouts. The latest year and month are incomplete, and small monthly samples can swing sharply. Chart tooltips show the number of rated cards.

These are UDisc round ratings, not PDGA ratings. Scorecards do not measure putt distance, drive accuracy, weather or why a score changed. The earlier snapshot remains available as a record of what I saw then; its counts and methods have been superseded by this update.
