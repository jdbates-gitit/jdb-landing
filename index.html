Daily Practice

Daily Discipline

I built a daily practice for recovery, reflection, and spiritual growth. One challenging question opens a lead reading, a companion, and a brief Echo from six public-domain works: Tao Te Ching, Chuang Tzu, Epictetus, Brother Lawrence, Psalms, and Heraclitus. Tao leads one day in four; Psalms is the companion every other day. I use Claude Haiku 4.5 to select the Echo and write connective notes, the Confluence, and a practice for the day: Notice, Surrender, Act. I added Return Tonight for an evening question and an optional browser-only note, plus a Seven-Day Trail of recent questions and practices. My workflow builds a fresh edition each morning.

Mortgage Ops · AI Pipeline

Mortgage Doc Classifier

I built a Claude-powered pipeline to watch an intake folder, classify W-2s, paystubs, and bank statements, extract structured fields, and route uncertain results to a person. I also added a basic annualized income projection from YTD data. My synthetic 20-document test returned 20/20 classifications and 85/85 gradable fields correctly—not proof of production accuracy. The model's confidence score still needs work.
