What I noticed

A par can tell two different stories.

A par after a drive that left me a birdie chance feels different from a par I had to scramble for. The scorecard records them the same way. Today, the opportunities stood out.

Three birdies in the first four holes. Four under on each nine. Birdies on the last two. I don't know yet whether running helped my rhythm. This is one round, but it's a useful place to start looking.

Next time, I want to remember roughly how far those birdie attempts were. That would help me tell the difference between getting closer and simply feeling more confident about the shot.
